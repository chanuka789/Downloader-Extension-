# MediaHarbor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

MediaHarbor is a browser extension that finds images and videos on the web page you are viewing and saves the files you choose to your computer.

**We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.**

- MediaHarbor has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.
- The extension reads the page you are viewing only inside your browser, to find images and videos. This information never leaves your device.
- The extension only connects to the websites you visit, to download the files you choose. On Instagram, it may ask Instagram for a post's media details using your existing login, only when you press a download button.
- Your settings (for example the download folder name and theme) are saved in your browser's extension storage. If you use Chrome Sync, Chrome may sync these settings between your own devices.
- The list of media found in a tab is stored locally in your browser and is deleted when the tab is closed or the browser restarts.

Please only download content you own or have permission to use.

Questions: contact the developer through the support link on the Chrome Web Store page.
